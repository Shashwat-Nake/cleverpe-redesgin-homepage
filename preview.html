Everything you need to know about CleverPe, our financing solutions, and partnerships.

CleverPe is an Indian digital affordability and fintech platform operated by CleverPe Technologies Private Limited. We bridge consumers and businesses with flexible credit solutions including Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL), No Cost EMI, EV mobility loans, healthcare financing, and merchant checkout integrations. In strict adherence to RBI guidelines, all credit lines and loan facilities are facilitated through our RBI-registered NBFC lending partners.
CleverPe provides five primary financing pillars: (1) Buy Now Pay Later for daily retail & gadgets, (2) No Cost EMI for electronics and appliances, (3) Electric Vehicle (EV) two-wheeler loans up to ₹2,00,000, (4) Healthcare and medical procedure financing at partner clinics, and (5) Insurance premium split-financing to convert annual lump sums into comfortable monthly instalments.
With No Cost EMI, you only repay the exact cash price of the product or service divided equally over your chosen tenure (e.g., 3, 6, 9, or 12 months). Any applicable interest or merchant discount is subsidized between our partner merchant and CleverPe, meaning you pay ₹0 additional interest, zero processing charges, and zero hidden penalties when paid on time.
Any Indian citizen aged 21 years or older with a valid Aadhaar, PAN card, active bank account, and regular source of income (salaried or self-employed) is eligible to apply. Our digital KYC check takes less than two minutes using your mobile number, providing an instant approval decision without requiring physical paperwork.
Merchants can integrate CleverPe via plug-and-play QR codes for physical retail stores, soundboxes, or our lightweight REST APIs & SDKs for e-commerce checkouts. Once partnered, merchants receive next-day upfront settlements directly into their bank accounts while CleverPe assumes all credit risk and instalment collection responsibilities.
Have a specific financing inquiry? Reach our Bangalore support desk at support@cleverpe.com
